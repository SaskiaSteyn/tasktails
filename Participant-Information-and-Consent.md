## PART 1 — PARTICIPANT INFORMATION SHEET

### 1. Invitation

You are invited to take part in a research study on how people engage with
gamified productivity applications. Please read this sheet carefully before
deciding. You may discuss it with others and ask us any questions before you
agree. Taking part is entirely your choice.

### 2. Who can take part

You may take part if you:

- are **18 years of age or older**;
- have a smartphone or computer with a current web browser and regular
  internet access;
- are willing to use a to-do-list application for your own real tasks over a
  period of two weeks.

### 3. What taking part involves

| When | What | Approximate time |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Create an account and complete a short entry questionnaire | [10] minutes |
| Two weeks | Use the TaskTails application for your own real, everyday tasks | At your own pace |
| End | Complete a short exit questionnaire | [10] minutes |
| End | Attend a debriefing session (see section 5) | [20] minutes |
| Optional | A short individual interview about your experience | [20–30] minutes |

You use the application as much or as little as suits you. There is no minimum
number of tasks and no daily requirement. Completing tasks in the application
earns in-game currency that you can spend in a virtual storefront on items for
a virtual pet.

By default the application may send you reminder and streak notifications. You
can switch these off at any time in the application's Settings screen.

### 4. What information we collect

**From the questionnaires and interview.** Your answers to the entry and exit
questionnaires, and, if you take part in one, your interview responses. With
your separate permission the interview may be audio-recorded so it can be
transcribed accurately; the recording is deleted once the transcript is
checked.

**From your account.** Your email address, the username and display name you
choose, and a profile photograph if you upload one. Your password is stored
only as an irreversible cryptographic hash and is never visible to us.

**From your use of the application.** The application records your activity as
research data. This includes:

- the tasks you create and complete, **including the text you type as the task
  title**, any subtasks, due dates, the difficulty level you assign, and the
  times at which tasks are created and completed;
- your in-game currency, experience, level, daily streak, and achievements;
- items you view, add to a cart, and purchase in the virtual storefront,
  including Lucky Box purchases and their contents;
- interactions with your virtual pets;
- interaction and session events — for example when you open the store, view an
  item, and when each session in the application starts and ends.

**Please treat the task titles you type as visible to the research team.** Do
not enter information you would prefer us not to read — for example medical
details, financial details, or other people's personal information. You can
describe a sensitive task in general terms ("appointment", "phone call")
without losing anything for the purposes of this study.

**Study group.** When your account is created you are assigned at random to one
of two versions of the application. You are not told which. Both versions are
fully functional; the differences are explained at the debriefing.

### 5. Partial disclosure and debriefing

**This study uses partial disclosure.** We have not told you the full, specific
focus of the research, because knowing it in advance would change the very
behaviour we are trying to measure and would make the results meaningless. We
have not told you anything untrue, and nothing is being withheld that would
affect your safety or your rights.

At the end of the two weeks you will attend a **debriefing session**, at which
we explain in full what the study was investigating, what the two versions of
the application differed in, and why the information had to be withheld. If you
cannot attend the session, we will provide the same explanation by email.

**After the debriefing you decide whether your data may be used.** If you say
no, everything collected from you is permanently deleted and is not analysed.
This decision costs you nothing — see section 6.

### 6. Thank-you gift and prize

As a thank-you for your time, every participant who takes part will receive
[state the gift and its approximate value, e.g. "a R50 airtime voucher"].

In addition, the participant at the top of the [state which leaderboard period:
"all-time"] leaderboard at the end of the two weeks will receive a **R500
Takealot voucher**.

**These are not conditional on the study getting your data.** If you withdraw
part-way through, or if you decide after the debriefing that your data may not
be used, you still receive the thank-you gift and you remain eligible for the
voucher on the same basis as everyone else. Withdrawing costs you nothing.

Please note that the leaderboard rewards activity in the application. You
should take part in the way that genuinely suits your own tasks and routine.
Please do not create tasks you would not otherwise have created in order to
improve your position.

### 7. Possible risks and discomfort

No risks beyond those of ordinary everyday app use are expected. The
application does not access your device's contacts, location, photos (other
than a profile picture you choose to upload), or any other application.

You may find some aspects of the interface mildly irritating, distracting, or
pressuring at times. This is expected, it is part of what the study examines,
and it will be explained at the debriefing. You are free to stop using the
application at any point if you find it uncomfortable.

Because the study involves recording your day-to-day task activity, there is
the ordinary privacy consideration that comes with that. Section 4 explains
what is recorded and section 11 explains how it is protected.

### 8. Benefits

There is no guaranteed direct benefit to you, though you may find the
application useful for organising your tasks. The study contributes to
understanding how gamified design features affect how people engage with
productivity software.

### 9. Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time, without
giving a reason and without penalty, and without any effect on your studies,
your standing, or your relationship with the University or with the
researchers.

To withdraw, simply email either researcher (section 14) and say that you wish
to stop. We will close your account, stop all data collection, and — unless you
tell us otherwise — permanently delete everything collected from you up to that
point. You do not have to explain why.

Once the analysis has been completed and the data fully anonymised
[state the point, e.g. "after [date]"], it may no longer be possible to
identify and remove your individual contribution.

### 10. Confidentiality — and what other participants can see

Your data are **pseudonymised**, not fully anonymous. Each participant is
assigned a random alphanumeric study code, and only that code appears in the
questionnaire data, the analysis, and the final report. The list linking study
codes to names and email addresses is stored separately from the research data,
is accessible only to the people named in section 12, and is destroyed once the
voucher has been delivered and the analysis is complete.

We need to hold this link for three reasons: to match your entry and exit
questionnaires to each other, to contact you about the interview and the
debriefing, and to deliver the thank-you gift and the voucher.

**The application includes a leaderboard that other participants can see.** It
displays your chosen username and your profile picture alongside your position
and score. Other participants can therefore see how you rank. If you would
rather not be recognisable, **choose a username that does not identify you and
do not upload a photograph of yourself** — nothing in the study requires
either. You can change your username in Settings at any time.

Nothing you type as a task title is ever shown to other participants.

### 11. How your information is stored and protected

Research data are held in a password-protected PostgreSQL database hosted on
[state where: e.g. "a University of Pretoria server" / "name of cloud provider
and country of hosting"]. Questionnaire responses, interview transcripts and
the study-code key are held on password-protected, encrypted drives accessible
only to the research team. Interview audio, where recorded, is stored in the
same way and deleted after transcription.

Your personal information is processed in accordance with the **Protection of
Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA)**. The lawful basis for processing
is your consent, which you may withdraw at any time as described in section 9.

Data will be retained for [number] years in line with University of Pretoria
policy, after which they will be permanently destroyed.

### 12. Who has access to your data

Access is limited to:

- the two student researchers named in section 14;
- the academic supervisor named in section 14;
- where required, the examiners of the project and the Research Ethics
  Committee, for verification purposes.

No one else is given access to identifiable data.

### 13. How the results will be used

The results will be written up as a [research report / dissertation] for
assessment, and may be presented at a conference or published in an academic
journal. Findings are reported in aggregate. No individual is identifiable in
any output. Where a direct quotation from an interview is used, it is
attributed only to a study code.
