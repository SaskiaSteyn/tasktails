Earning Cooldown — UI mock

Companion to ADDENDUM-earning-cooldown.md (issue #224). Sample states for the task screen, the dashboard status pill, and the admin telemetry panel. All numbers are illustrative. Tokens match design_handoff/README.md.

1 · On the task screen

There is no floating "pop" card. Today, when you tick a task off, that row’s right-hand coin figure briefly swaps from the tier preview to the real granted amount (+35 · +45 XP) and the checkbox scales up — that’s the whole "reward pop", and it stays exactly as-is. The cooldown adds one thing: a violet inline banner at the top of the task list (src/components/tasks/task-list.tsx), reusing the same shape and slide-in as the green onboarding-goal banner already there. Window progress (2 / 3) lives on the header pill in §2, not on the row.
