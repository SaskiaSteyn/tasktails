Turn 1 · Lucky boxes Four boxes, bought like items, opened from your own shelf Boxes are bought in the Store like any StoreItemCard, then wait unopened on a My boxes screen reached from the store header. Opening is a tap → pop → fan of face-down cards you flip one at a time. Rarity vocabulary and tints are the codebase's four tiers; odds are the real RARITY_ODDS from gacha.ts (55 / 30 / 12 / 3). Shiny is new and only ever comes out of a box.
Flag · pricing The ladder now reads 50 / 120 / 200 / 300 for 1 / 3 / 5 / 7 items — 50, 40, 40 and ~43 coins per item, so Lucky Trove is fractionally worse value than Lucky Haul. Either price the Trove at 280 or leave the premium as the price of the bigger single-session open.
Question for dev StoreItemCategory is FOOD / ACCESSORIES / DECORATIONS / ANIMALS, and a box is none of them. It needs a fifth category (or its own table) plus an unopened-box row per user — gacha.ts today spends coins and grants an item in one transaction, with nothing owned in between.
